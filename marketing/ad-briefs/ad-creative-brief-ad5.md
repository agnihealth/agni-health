## Rationale for Family History Angle

The pilot's winning ad (Ad 4 — "Take control of your blood sugar") speaks to men who already *know* something is off.

Ad 5 targets a complementary psychographic: the man who doesn't have symptoms yet, but has a parent or sibling with diabetes. This is a large, high-intent segment in the South Asian male 35–55 demographic — a family history of diabetes is one of the strongest predictors of Ad engagement in chronic disease audiences.

A/B testing Ads 4 and 5 together will help us understand:
- Do anxiety-about-numbers (Ad 4) vs. family-legacy (Ad 5) frames have different conversion rates?
- Are they reaching different sub-segments within our SA male audience?
