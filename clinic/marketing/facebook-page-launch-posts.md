📊 The South Asian diabetes paradox — in numbers:

• South Asians are **4x more likely** to develop type 2 diabetes than white Americans
• We develop it at a **BMI of 23** — not the standard cutoff of 30
• **1 in 3 South Asian men over 40** meets criteria for metabolic syndrome
• The waist circumference risk threshold for SA men is **90 cm** — not the 102 cm used in most US guidelines

Translation: if you're South Asian and your doctor told you your weight is "fine," your A1c is "borderline," and your waist is "a little high" — you may be under-screened.

This isn't your doctor's fault. It's a data gap that standard clinical training doesn't fully address.

At Agni Health, we screen and treat to the right thresholds for our population.
