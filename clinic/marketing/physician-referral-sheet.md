## Who We Serve

South Asian males (30–65) with:
- Prediabetes (A1c 5.7–6.4%) or early Type 2 diabetes (A1c 6.5–8%)
- Metabolic syndrome
- Obesity / overweight with visceral adiposity
- Insulin resistance (elevated fasting insulin / HOMA-IR)
- Family history of T2DM or cardiovascular disease in a South Asian relative

**We do not serve**: Active T2DM on insulin, emergent conditions, non-metabolic primary care needs

---

## Why Agni Health?

**South Asians require different thresholds.**  
They develop type 2 diabetes 4x more often than white Americans, at lower BMIs (risk starts at 23 vs. 30 standard cutoff), and at younger ages. Standard metabolic screening often misses them until disease is established.

**What we offer that standard primary care often cannot:**
- 30-minute initial consultation + full SA-specific metabolic workup
- HOMA-IR, ApoB, hsCRP, Lp(a), ALT (NAFLD screen)
- Waist circumference assessed to SA threshold (>90cm, not >102cm)
- Individualized lifestyle + medication protocol (GLP-1 where indicated, FDA-approved)
- Ongoing physician-led async care model with regular follow-up
- No insurance billing (self-pay): removes referral complexity
