## Executive Summary

This pilot reveals a **promising top-of-funnel** but a **broken middle-funnel**. The ads work—we're getting cheap, qualified clicks. The product isn't the problem. The homepage is hemorrhaging 96% of paid traffic before they even see what we're selling.

**Bottom line:** Don't pause ads. Fix the homepage conversion NOW while continuing to learn from paid traffic.

## 3. Funnel Priority — Where to Focus First

### The Data Is Unambiguous: Fix the Homepage

```
Funnel Conversion Rates:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Ad → Click:         5.79%  ✅ Excellent (4x industry avg)
Click → LPV:        39.1%  ⚠️ Acceptable (landing page loads)
LPV → /start:       4.3%   🚨 PROBLEM (should be 15-25%)
/start → /book:     36.4%  ✅ Decent (intent filtering)
/book → Lead:       25.0%  ⚠️ Acceptable (form completion)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

**The single biggest lever:** Homepage → /start conversion.

If we 5x that (4.3% → 20%), holding everything else constant:
- 1,269 LPVs → 254 /start views → 92 /book → 23 leads
- That's $32/lead instead of $149/lead
- With 20% close rate: 4-5 customers from this pilot alone

### Specific Homepage Fixes (Priority Order)

1. **Above-the-fold CTA clarity** — What do I do? Why now? (Current likely buries the action)
2. **Social proof near CTA** — Testimonial or "Join 500+ members" adjacent to button
3. **Value proposition in 8 words or less** — "Reverse metabolic dysfunction in 90 days"
4. **Remove friction** — If requiring email before showing /start, don't
5. **Mobile optimization** — Likely 70%+ of Meta traffic is mobile

### Do NOT Focus On Yet

- **Ads** — They're working. Ad 6 is a clear winner to reallocate budget to, but don't rewrite creative yet.
- **Follow-up sequences** — You need more than 5 leads to optimize email/SMS cadence.
- **Checkout optimization** — Need more checkout initiations to diagnose friction.

## 4. Go/No-Go Recommendation

### ✅ GO — But With Conditions

**Recommendation:** Run next campaign in parallel with homepage fixes.

| Action | Timeline | Budget |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Homepage V2 live | 3-5 days | $0 (Sohan's time) |
| Continue ads at $25/day | Immediately | $175/week |
| Kill underperformers | Now | Reallocate to Ad 6 |
| Dedicated landing page test | Day 7-14 | $200 split test |

### Why Not Pause?

1. **Learning velocity** — Paid traffic = fast iteration cycles. Organic is too slow for a pilot.
2. **Audience building** — Meta pixel is learning. Stopping resets the algorithm.
3. **Cheap traffic** — At $0.23 CPC, you're essentially renting 1,500 visitors/week for $350. That's invaluable user research.
4. **Seasonal timing** — May/June is pre-summer health motivation. Don't miss the window.

**Reallocate to:**
